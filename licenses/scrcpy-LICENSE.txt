Genymobile/scrcpy & Android Platform Tools (ADB) Attribution
============================================================

PocketDeck optionally invokes `adb` (Android SDK Platform-Tools) and `scrcpy-server`
from Genymobile/scrcpy (https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy, v5.0.1) for zero-install
USB and Android 11+ Wireless Debugging screen mirroring.
